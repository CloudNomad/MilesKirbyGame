# 🎮 Kirby x Spider-Man: Miles Morales – ESL Adventure  
## Level 1: The Three Doors Challenge 🚪

---

## 🧩 Game Overview

Welcome to **Kirby x Spider-Man: Miles Morales – ESL Adventure**, a fun and interactive 2D learning game designed to help ESL students improve their **grammar, vocabulary, and math skills** from **Grades 1 through 6**.

In this exciting crossover world, players control a character inspired by **Kirby and Miles Morales**, combining curiosity, agility, and problem-solving skills to progress through each level.

---

## 🎯 Level 1 Objective

In **Level 1**, the player begins on the **left side of the screen** and enters the stage by moving right.

Your goal is to:
- Explore the level
- Answer questions correctly
- Choose the correct path
- Reach the correct door to advance

---

## 🚪 The Three Doors

At the center of the level, you will find:

- **Door 1**
- **Door 2**
- **Door 3**

Each door represents a possible outcome based on your answers.

### 🧠 How It Works:
- You will be given **ESL-based questions** (grammar, vocabulary, or math).
- Based on your answers, you must choose the correct door.
- Only **one door leads to the next level**.
- Incorrect choices may require retrying or redirecting.

---

## 💎 Bonus System

In the middle of the stage, you may find a **BONUS item (gem)**.

### ⭐ Bonus Round:
- Triggered when collected
- Offers a special challenge
- If you win:
  - You can **skip a level** or gain a major advantage
- If you lose:
  - You continue the level as normal

---

## 📚 Learning Focus (Level 1)

Level 1 focuses on foundational ESL skills:

### ✏️ Grammar
- Basic sentence structure
- Simple verb usage

### 📖 Vocabulary
- Common everyday words
- Word recognition

### ➕ Math
- Basic arithmetic (addition, subtraction)

## 🚀 Progression System

As players advance through the game:

- 🔓 More **levels** will unlock
- 🚪 More **doors** will appear
- 🔑 Some doors will require **keys**
- 🧠 Questions will become **more challenging**
- 💎 Bonus rounds will offer bigger rewards
